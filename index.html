    What happens to a call

    1. Transcribe.

      Whisper, fine-tuned on helpdesk recordings, writes Hindi and Hinglish in Latin script, the way the portal shows it.

    2. Split the speakers.

      Each line is labelled agent or candidate from the two sides of the call.

    3. Pick the category.

      A model trained on about 7,200 calls Gemini had labelled combines a text classifier, embeddings and the most similar past calls, and reports how sure it is.

    4. Fill the QA sheet.

      A small open-weight LLM writes the insight and summary and scores the agent on greeting, understanding, correctness, next steps and closing.

    5. Wake only when needed.

      The GPU server sleeps when idle. The portal wakes it through AWS Lambda once calls queue up, and one setting switches back to Gemini.
